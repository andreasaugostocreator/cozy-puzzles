Cozy logic puzzles

Campsite & Slide Out

Two logic puzzles with endless generated levels. Your progress stays on this phone.

Campsite

Pitch one tent beside every tree. Tents never touch, and the numbers count tents per row and column.

Slide Out

Tap an arrow and it slides off the board along its head. Blocked arrows cost a heart.

To play it like an app: tap Share in Safari, then “Add to Home Screen”.
